Additional issues discovered during repair
The runtime settings parser dropped N8N keys containing digits and sometimes retained a leading equals sign. Credential references now load correctly and missing required references fail immediately.
n8n's signed Wait URL and its internal-origin expression needed compatible validation. Both were verified live, not just with a standalone JavaScript mock.
Notification preferences originally allowed the demo account to bypass opt-out. Consent is now checked at both queue and claim, and preference-only edits do not unnecessarily requeue matching.
Errors can occur before a match exists. Only registered, opted-in error notifications may omit match_id; no other action may do so. The actions owner also references auth.users with cascade deletion.
A safe calendar retry failed after a fresh evaluation because it kept its old revision. Migration 0021 now checks current owned policy, refreshes the private payload, preserves stable identity and excludes email retries. The actual retry updated the existing event successfully.
The older gateway allowed arbitrary webhook and Wait paths. It now uses a strict two-endpoint POST allowlist, tested locally and through the live HTTPS tunnel.
