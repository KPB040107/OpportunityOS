Slice 3 policy version `decision-v1`, semantic contract `match-v1`. The guide fixes the score weights and autonomy thresholds; the rubrics and confidence formula below are implementation choices frozen against `testdata/slice3-fixtures.json`.

Hard checks use literal source requirements only. A confirmed failure is INELIGIBLE; otherwise any unresolved mandatory requirement, empty extracted requirements list or missing deadline is UNKNOWN. Literal recognized branch/degree lists and explicitly listed year alternatives are checked in code; broad descriptions and year ranges remain UNKNOWN. Experience qualifiers and mandatory residence cannot be verified from the current profile and stay UNKNOWN. Preferred cities do not prove residence. Contradictory hard facts require clarification; redundant soft context and quotation formatting do not create a conflict. A date-only deadline before the current Asia/Kolkata date is expired. A deadline today has no verified cutoff time and stays UNKNOWN. UTC is used for saved timestamps.

Score out of 100: skill fit 40 (validated semantic fit); career alignment 20 (validated semantic fit); interest 15 (exact opportunity type in interests); location 10 (normalized exact city or explicitly remote against preferences); academic profile fit 10 (fraction of stated degree/branch/year checks that pass); urgency 5 (1 for 1–7 days, .75 for 8–14, .5 for 15–30, .25 beyond 30). Unknown components are displayed as unknown, contribute zero points and do not redistribute their weights. Semantic evidence must quote both the supplied opportunity evidence and the relevant profile skill/goal. Missing skills/goals cannot earn semantic points.

Confidence = min(model confidence, .30 completeness + .30 verified evidence + .25 clarity + .15 model confidence). Completeness is the fraction present among title, organization, deadline and extracted requirements. Verified evidence is coverage of present title, organization, deadline, location, type and skills by Slice 2 validated quotes. Clarity = max(0, 1 − .2 × ambiguity count). Model confidence is the minimum of extraction and matching confidence. The cap prevents complete fields from outweighing explicit model uncertainty. Security annotations (`source_contains_instructions`) do not reduce confidence or change eligibility. Real conflicting facts do. Numeric results are saved to four decimal places; comparisons use that saved precision.

Policy order: expired → IGNORE; INELIGIBLE → IGNORE; UNKNOWN → ASK regardless of score; eligible score below 40 → IGNORE; otherwise ACT only at both mode thresholds (Cautious 80/.85, Balanced 70/.75, Autopilot 60/.65), else ASK. Ineligible and expired cases skip matching and display no invented score/confidence. Failed or quota-limited matching has no decision. There is no standing-rule override in this slice.

The exact same factory is embedded in the n8n Code nodes and imported by tests. The model cannot return policy fields or use tools. A visible Switch routes saved current decisions. ACT and ASK are recorded readiness/review decisions only; side effects and authenticated approval handling belong to Slice 4.

Each job is atomically claimed, fenced by token/revision and the evaluation date. Profile, skill, requirement or material opportunity changes enqueue reevaluation and invalidate older runs. Mode and daily urgency changes reuse matching evidence. Matching is cached per owner and minimal profile/fact/model/prompt version; identity and credentials are excluded. Extraction and matching share the existing serialized seven-attempt reservations, 60-second spacing and 350-per-Pacific-day ceiling. A failed evaluation needs an explicit bounded retry. The SQL schema exposes only owned public decision columns and keeps claims/snapshots/cache/budget private.
